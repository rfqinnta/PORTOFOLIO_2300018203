APPLICATION LETTER POSITION: UI/UX DESIGNER • TECHNOVA DIGITAL SOLUTIONS

Dear HR Manager,

I would like to apply for the UI/UX Designer position at TechNova Digital Solutions. I study Informatics at Universitas Ahmad Dahlan, and what I enjoy most is the moment a rough sketch becomes something people can actually click through and use.

I do most of my design work in Figma, where I build wireframes, design systems, and interactive prototypes. Before designing a screen, I try to map the user’s journey and organise the information so nothing important is hard to find. I also write the front end myself with HTML, CSS, and Tailwind, and I have worked with PHP, Laravel, QGIS, and Git in my projects.

This semester I did not do a formal internship, so my experience comes from two academic projects. In the first, I designed a way for students to register for Student Activity Units (UKM) at my campus. Instead of a plain form, I built a showcase website with real event photos and bold typography, so students can see what each unit is like before they join. In the second, Pangkalpinang Beach Locations WebGIS, I designed an interactive map with custom pin markers and location cards. The main challenge was keeping the map clean and easy to read, so each card shows only the essential details.

Your announcement asks for basic technical skills and project experience. These two projects show that I can design with users in mind and also build my own designs, which should make it easier for me to work with your developers.

My portfolio is available right on this page — feel free to jump straight to the showcase banner below.

Thank you for your time. I look forward to hearing from you.

Yours sincerely,

Muhammad Rifqi Ananta
